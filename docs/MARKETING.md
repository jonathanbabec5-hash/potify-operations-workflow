# POTIFY Operations Workflow marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Editorial tool developers and creator operations teams. **Offer.** Let an editor prove the difference between a reviewed draft and a verified publication.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish a before-and-after test for a draft edit that revokes approval. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Run the state-transition fixtures. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 3 editorial teams run the examples. |

### Sales test

Run a paid editorial workflow workshop only after a buyer supplies a sample failure with permission. Target one proposal accepted against a specific process improvement. Qualify one team for a paid setup workshop after it identifies a real review bottleneck.

### Measurement and decision

Use `operations-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** The private operations app, imported tasks, comments and production publishing records stay separate. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
