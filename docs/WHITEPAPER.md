# POTIFY Operations Workflow
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Public approval-state library**

### Goal and audience

Let an editor prove the difference between a reviewed draft and a verified publication. The immediate audience is editorial tool developers and creator operations teams.

### Design and implementation

Pure transitions govern review, schedule evidence, publication and revision. Tests operate on synthetic items without a publishing connection. Keep an approval attached to exact copy and media. A substantive edit must return the item to review even when the planned slot stays the same.

### What a contributor can establish

All seven original workflow tests passed. They cover approval scope, revisions, required schedule evidence and publication receipts. A scheduled timestamp without platform evidence cannot establish dispatch. A publication URL and a delivery receipt answer different operational questions.

### Evidence and outstanding work

The private operations app, imported tasks, comments and production publishing records stay separate. The next acceptance case is concrete. Run fixtures proving that editing approved content invalidates approval and a schedule requires a receipt.

### Place in POTIFY

Qualify one team for a paid setup workshop after it identifies a real review bottleneck. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
